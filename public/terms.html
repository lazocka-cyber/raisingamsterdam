What RaisingAmsterdam is

RaisingAmsterdam (raisingamsterdam.com) is run by Zdenka Lazokova, Amsterdam. It is a place where families and babysitters or other service providers in Amsterdam can find each other, meet up and help each other. We are a noticeboard, not an agency: we do not employ, check or recommend the babysitters or services listed, and we are not a party to any arrangement you make with someone you meet here.

Your account

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • Use your own email address and keep access to it safe.
  • You can delete your account at any time on the Delete account page.

What you post

  • Be honest. Listings, meetups, SOS requests and reviews must be truthful and about you or your own experience.
  • No spam, no hate, nothing illegal, and no personal details of children such as full names or recognisable photos.
  • You keep ownership of what you post. You allow us to show it on RaisingAmsterdam for as long as it is online.
  • We may remove content or accounts that break these rules.

Meeting people from the site

Please take the usual care: meet first, ask for references, and check any documents (for example a VOG certificate of conduct) yourself. Any agreement about work, pay or care is between you and the other person.
