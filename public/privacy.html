🔒 Your data

Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026. In short: we only collect what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account yourself at any time.

Who we are

RaisingAmsterdam (raisingamsterdam.com) is a community app for families in Amsterdam: babysitter and service listings, meetups, an SOS board and guides. The controller responsible for your data is Zdenka Lazokova, based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Contact: raisingsmall.info@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive the name and email of your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Your listing (if you post one): what you fill in, such as title, description, price, age groups, WhatsApp number, postcode, neighbourhoods, languages and an optional photo. Your listing is public: anyone visiting the site can see it.
  • Meetups, SOS requests, reviews and ratings you post. These are visible to other users.
  • Membership: if you buy access, payment is handled by Gumroad. We only check your licence key with Gumroad. We never see your card details.
  • Push notifications: if you turn them on, we store the technical subscription your browser gives us so we can send you SOS alerts.
  • How you found us: when you arrive through a link with campaign tags (for example from Facebook), we store that source with your account, so we know which posts help families find us.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider automatically processes limited technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the site.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in (performing our agreement with you).
  • To show your listing, meetups and SOS requests to other families (performing our agreement with you).
  • To send you sign-in emails and, if you asked for them, notifications.
  • To understand which of our posts and ads bring people to the site (our legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for anything else.

Who helps us run the app

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Vercel: hosting, and Vercel Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies.
  • Resend (with Amazon SES): sends our emails.
  • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google”.
  • Gumroad: payments and licence keys for membership.
  • Meta (Facebook): we use the Meta Pixel to measure visits and sign-ups that come from our Facebook and Instagram posts and ads. Meta may use cookies for this.
  • YouTube: our tutorial video only loads from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) after you tap play.

Some of these companies may process data outside the EU. They do so under the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile and listings are deleted. Uploaded listing photos are removed on request, just email us. Our email provider keeps a log of sent emails for a limited period.

Your rights

You can see, correct and delete your data. Delete your account any time on the Delete account page. For anything else (a copy of your data, a correction, an objection) email raisingsmall.info@gmail.com. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

RaisingAmsterdam is for adults. Please do not post personal details of children, such as full names or photos where a child can be recognised.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page.
