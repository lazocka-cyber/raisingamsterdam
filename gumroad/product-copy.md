## Full description

### 🌍 Made for expat parents in Amsterdam
You moved here for work, for love, for adventure — but nobody handed you a manual for raising kids in the Netherlands. RaisingAmsterdam is that manual, plus the community to go with it.

### 🍼 Find a babysitter you can trust
Browse babysitters, local family services and community groups in one place. Read real reviews from other parents, see who speaks your language, and reach them directly on WhatsApp — no middlemen, no waiting.

### 🚨 Stuck without a sitter? Post an SOS
Something came up and you need someone *tonight*? Post an urgent SOS request and nearby babysitters get pinged right away. Browsing and helping out is always free — posting an SOS is a member perk.

### 👋 Meet other parents nearby
The hardest part of expat life is finding your people. Plan a casual meetup — a coffee, a walk, a playdate — or just say hi and let nearby families reach out. Real friendships start here.

### 📖 Find your way around the Dutch system
Health insurance, the consultatiebureau, kraamzorg, childcare and toeslag, choosing a basisschool, the swimming diploma, the paperwork that gets you started — explained in plain English, the way a friend would tell you. Plus a guide on child first aid (kinder-EHBO) and which complementary care your insurance may reimburse.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is this a subscription?**
No. It's a one-time payment for full access. No recurring charges, ever.

**What do I get for free without a membership?**
Plenty — browse every listing and review, read all the Guides, and use the Meet board. Membership unlocks *contacting* providers directly and posting SOS requests.

**How do I get access after paying?**
You'll receive an access key. Open the app, go to the Membership page, paste your key, and you're unlocked instantly.

**Which devices does it work on?**
RaisingAmsterdam is a web app that works on any phone or computer — and you can add it to your home screen like a normal app.

**Is it only for Amsterdam?**
For now, yes — built for families in and around Amsterdam. More cities are coming.

**Is the Guides content official advice?**
No — it's a friendly starting point to help you find your way. Always confirm details with the official source, your insurer or the gemeente. We can't accept responsibility for decisions made from it.
